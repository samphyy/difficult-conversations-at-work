## Screenshot provenance

The three images in `assets/` are selected Quest captures from the existing Meta store screenshot set, also used on the public app information page:

- `learn-with-rowan.png`: recorded lesson and playback controls; the seated character is Alex, not Rowan.
- `scenario.png`: the fictional missed-deadlines scenario briefing.
- `preparation-card.png`: example opening based on facts and impact.

These images were captured in Build 36. They illustrate the learning experience and are not screenshots of every feature in the current release. No private learner conversations, account records, or purchase details are included.
